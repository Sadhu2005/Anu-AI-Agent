## Goal

Prove that Safe Mutation and the Task Engine stay correct under adverse
conditions: corrupted checkpoints, concurrent access, multi-file changes, and
exhausted retries.

## Changes

foundation (see [Foundation](v0.5.1-foundation.md)) plus the V0.5.2 tests.
